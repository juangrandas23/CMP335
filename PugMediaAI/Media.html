Companion Breed

Pug

A cheerful, affectionate little dog with a playful personality and a big heart.

General Information

"Pugs can be wonderful, loving companions, but they require careful consideration regarding their health needs and clingy personalities."

Health problems: Because they are a flat-faced (brachycephalic) breed, Pugs often have breathing troubles, eye sensitivities, and skin fold infections. They also struggle in hot or humid weather.
